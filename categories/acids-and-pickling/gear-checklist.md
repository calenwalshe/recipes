# Acids & Pickling Gear Checklist

Essential items for your pickled or fermented signatures:

- White vinegar
- Apple cider vinegar
- Rice vinegar
- Citrus powder or dehydrated lime
- Pickling salt or kosher salt
- Whole spices for brines:
  - Bay leaf
  - Peppercorn
  - Coriander seed
  - Mustard seed
  - Cumin seed
