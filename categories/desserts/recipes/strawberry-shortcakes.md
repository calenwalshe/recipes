# Strawberry Shortcakes

## Overview
- **Category:** Dessert
- **Prep Time:** 25 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 15 minutes
- **Total Time:** 40 minutes
- **Servings:** 6 shortcakes

## Ingredients
### Macerated Strawberries
- 1 pound (450 g) fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
- 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice

### Sweet Biscuits
- 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1/2 cup (115 g) cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 2/3 cup (160 ml) cold heavy cream, plus more for brushing
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- Turbinado sugar for sprinkling (optional)

### Whipped Cream
- 1 cup (240 ml) cold heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

## Equipment
- Mixing bowls
- Pastry cutter or two forks
- Baking sheet
- Parchment paper
- Biscuit cutter or 3-inch round cutter
- Hand mixer or whisk

## Instructions
1. Combine strawberries, granulated sugar, and lemon juice in a bowl. Toss to coat and let sit for at least 20 minutes to release juices.
2. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Cut in cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces.
4. Whisk cream, egg, and vanilla in a separate bowl. Pour over the flour mixture and stir gently until a shaggy dough forms.
5. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and pat into a 3/4-inch-thick rectangle. Fold in half, pat out again, and repeat twice more for flaky layers.
6. Cut six rounds with a biscuit cutter, pressing straight down without twisting. Place on the prepared baking sheet and brush tops with cream. Sprinkle with turbinado sugar if desired.
7. Bake 12–15 minutes until biscuits are puffed and golden. Cool on a wire rack for 10 minutes.
8. Whip cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla to soft peaks.
9. Split biscuits horizontally. Spoon macerated strawberries and their juices over the bottom halves, dollop with whipped cream, and cap with the biscuit tops.

## Make-Ahead and Storage
- Biscuits can be baked earlier in the day; warm briefly before serving.
- Store leftover components separately in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.

## Notes
- Keep ingredients cold for tender biscuits; refrigerate the bowl and pastry cutter for best results.

## Variations
- Substitute peaches or mixed berries for a seasonal twist.
- Add 1/2 teaspoon finely grated lemon zest to the biscuit dough for brightness.
