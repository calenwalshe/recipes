# Classic Chocolate Cake

## Overview
- **Category:** Dessert
- **Prep Time:** 25 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 30 minutes
- **Total Time:** 55 minutes
- **Servings:** 10 slices

## Ingredients
- 1 3/4 cups (220 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 cups (400 g) granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup (75 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 cup (240 ml) buttermilk, room temperature
- 1 cup (240 ml) freshly brewed coffee, hot
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) neutral oil (canola or vegetable)
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract

### Chocolate Buttercream
- 1 cup (225 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3 cups (360 g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 3/4 cup (75 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract

## Equipment
- Two 9-inch round cake pans
- Stand mixer or hand mixer
- Mixing bowls
- Rubber spatula
- Cooling rack
- Offset spatula for frosting

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour the cake pans, then line the bottoms with parchment circles.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, baking powder, and salt until evenly combined.
3. Add eggs, buttermilk, oil, and vanilla. Mix on medium speed for 2 minutes, scraping down the sides as needed.
4. Reduce speed to low and carefully pour in the hot coffee. Mix until the batter is smooth; it will be thin.
5. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans. Bake for 28–32 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
6. Cool the cakes in the pans for 10 minutes, then turn onto a wire rack to cool completely.
7. For the buttercream, beat the butter on medium-high speed until creamy. Sift together powdered sugar, cocoa powder, and salt, then add to the butter in three additions, mixing on low.
8. Pour in the heavy cream and vanilla. Increase speed to medium-high and whip for 2 minutes until fluffy.
9. Level the cooled cake layers if needed. Place one layer on a serving plate, spread with buttercream, and top with the second layer. Frost the top and sides, creating swirls with an offset spatula.

## Make-Ahead and Storage
- Wrap unfrosted cake layers tightly and store at room temperature for up to 1 day or freeze for up to 2 months.
- Frosted cake keeps refrigerated for up to 4 days; bring to room temperature before serving.

## Notes
- Hot coffee deepens the chocolate flavor; substitute hot water if preferred.

## Variations
- Add 1 cup (170 g) mini chocolate chips to the batter for extra texture.
- Fill with raspberry jam between layers for a fruity twist.
