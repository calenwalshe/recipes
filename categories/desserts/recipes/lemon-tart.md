# Lemon Tart with Buttery Crust

## Overview
- **Category:** Dessert
- **Prep Time:** 30 minutes (plus chilling)
- **Cook Time:** 35 minutes
- **Total Time:** 1 hour 5 minutes
- **Servings:** 8 slices

## Ingredients
### Crust
- 1 1/4 cups (160 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup (30 g) powdered sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 1/2 cup (115 g) cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1–2 tablespoons ice water

### Lemon Filling
- 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 2 large egg yolks
- 2/3 cup (160 ml) freshly squeezed lemon juice (about 4 lemons)
- 1 tablespoon finely grated lemon zest
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream
- Pinch of fine sea salt

## Equipment
- 9-inch tart pan with removable bottom
- Food processor or pastry cutter
- Mixing bowls and whisk
- Saucepan
- Fine-mesh strainer

## Instructions
1. In a food processor, pulse flour, powdered sugar, and salt to combine. Add cold butter and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
2. Add egg yolk and pulse again, drizzling in ice water until the dough just comes together when pinched.
3. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface, press into a disk, wrap, and chill for 30 minutes.
4. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Roll out the chilled dough to a 12-inch circle and fit into the tart pan. Trim excess dough and prick the bottom with a fork.
5. Line the crust with parchment and fill with pie weights. Bake for 18 minutes, then remove weights and parchment and bake 5 more minutes until lightly golden. Cool on a rack while preparing filling.
6. Reduce oven temperature to 325°F (160°C). In a saucepan, whisk together sugar, eggs, egg yolks, lemon juice, zest, cream, and salt. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, 6–8 minutes. Do not let it boil.
7. Pour the custard through a fine-mesh strainer into the baked crust to remove any zest or cooked egg bits.
8. Bake the tart for 12–15 minutes, until the filling is set around the edges but still slightly wobbly in the center.
9. Cool to room temperature, then chill for at least 2 hours. Garnish with powdered sugar or whipped cream before slicing.

## Make-Ahead and Storage
- Tart can be baked a day ahead; keep covered in the refrigerator.
- Leftovers keep refrigerated for up to 3 days.

## Notes
- For a crisp crust, brush with beaten egg white after blind baking and before filling.

## Variations
- Swap half the lemon juice for fresh lime juice for a citrus twist.
- Top with fresh berries for added color and flavor.
